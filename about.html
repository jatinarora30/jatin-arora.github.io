About

I'm a Robotics Software Engineer at Addverb Technologies, where I've spent over two years developing and deploying autonomous mobile robots. My work spans ROS-based localization, hardware interfaces, state machines, navigation, control, and system identification, and I've supported the deployment of more than 200 robots across industrial sites.

I completed an M.S. (Research) in Robotics and Control Theory at IIT Delhi (2024–2026), where my thesis focused on balancing safety and optimality in robot path planning. My research interests span perception, planning, control, and learning.

I write about what I'm building and learning on Loose Notes, and you can book a 1:1 session with me on Topmate.
